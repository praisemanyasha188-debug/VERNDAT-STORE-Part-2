Our Story

Rooted in a love for living spaces

Verdant began in 2017 as a small weekend market stall run by two plant lovers in Cape Town. Today we are a proudly South African online and in-store retailer with a mission to make sustainable, nature-inspired living accessible to everyone.

Every product we carry is either locally grown, locally made, or ethically sourced — because we believe beautiful spaces and a healthy planet go hand in hand.
