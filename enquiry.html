Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard nationwide delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express next-day delivery is available in Cape Town and Johannesburg for an additional fee.

Do you offer plant care guarantees?

Yes. We offer a 14-day health guarantee on all live plants. If your plant arrives damaged or becomes unhealthy within 14 days, contact us for a replacement or refund.

Can I visit a store before ordering?

Absolutely. Our Cape Town store is open Monday to Saturday 9am to 5pm and our Johannesburg store Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm, Saturday 9am to 4pm.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes. We offer premium gift wrapping with a handwritten message for all orders. Simply mention it in your enquiry or add a note during checkout.

What is the minimum for bulk orders?

Bulk order discounts start from 10 units. Corporate accounts with recurring orders receive additional benefits. Enquire for details.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we deliver within South Africa only. Due to plant import regulations, international shipping is not available at this time.
