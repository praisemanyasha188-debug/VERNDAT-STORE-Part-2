Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard nationwide delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express next-day delivery is available in Cape Town and Johannesburg for an additional fee.

Do you offer plant care guarantees?

Yes! We offer a 14-day health guarantee on all live plants. If your plant arrives damaged or becomes unhealthy within 14 days of delivery, contact us for a replacement or refund.

Can I visit a store before ordering?

Absolutely. Our Cape Town store is open Mon–Sat 9am–5pm and our Johannesburg store Mon–Fri 9am–6pm, Sat 9am–4pm.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes — we offer premium gift wrapping with a handwritten message for all orders. Simply add a note during checkout or mention it in your enquiry.

What's the minimum for bulk orders?

Bulk order discounts start from 10 units. Corporate accounts with recurring orders receive additional benefits — enquire for details.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we deliver within South Africa only. Due to plant import regulations, international shipping is not available at this time.
