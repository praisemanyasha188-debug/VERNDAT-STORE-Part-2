2017 Founded
How it started

From a weekend stall to a national store

Verdant was born at the Oranjezicht City Farm Market in Cape Town, where co-founders Yolanda Petersen and Sipho Dlamini spent every Saturday selling potted plants they propagated from their own gardens.

What started as a side passion quickly grew into something bigger. Customers kept coming back — not just for the plants, but for the care guides, the repotting tips, and the genuine love for greenery that Yolanda and Sipho brought to every interaction.

In 2019, they opened their first physical store in Cape Town's Gardens neighbourhood. Today, Verdant operates two stores and a thriving online shop, serving customers across South Africa with the same warmth and expertise that started it all.

🎯 Our Mission

To make nature-inspired living accessible to every South African home, through ethically sourced plants and handcrafted goods that last a lifetime.

🌍 Our Vision

A South Africa where every home has a thriving indoor garden, where communities grow together, and where sustainable consumption is the norm.

What we stand for

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do — from how we source our products to how we talk to customers.

🌱

Sustainability

Every product we sell is either locally grown, locally handmade, or sourced from suppliers who share our commitment to environmental responsibility.

🤝

Community

We partner with local growers, artists, and small producers — keeping money in our communities and celebrating South African craftsmanship.

💚

Education

We include detailed care guides with every plant purchase and host free monthly workshops for beginner and intermediate plant parents.

✨

Quality

We never compromise on quality. Every plant is inspected before shipping and every artisan product is vetted by our in-house curation team.

💬

Transparency

We're honest about where our products come from, how they're priced, and what happens if something goes wrong. No hidden costs or fine print.

🎁

Delight

Every Verdant order is packaged beautifully and includes a handwritten care note. We want unboxing a Verdant delivery to feel like receiving a gift.

Our Journey

How we got here

2017

The First Stall

Yolanda and Sipho debut at the Oranjezicht City Farm Market with 30 hand-potted houseplants. They sell out in two hours.

2018

Online Launch

verdant.co.za goes live. First online order arrives from Durban within 24 hours of launch.

2019

First Brick & Mortar

Verdant opens its first physical store at 32 Blossom Lane, Gardens, Cape Town — a converted Victorian cottage with a working greenhouse.

2022

Johannesburg Expansion

A second store opens in Rosebank, Johannesburg, serving the growing northern demand. The team grows to 12 full-time staff.

2024

Sustainability Certification

Verdant achieves full Fair Trade and sustainable sourcing certification across its entire product range.

2026

5 000 Customers Milestone

Verdant celebrates serving over 5 000 happy customers nationwide — and launches its first plant subscription box service.
